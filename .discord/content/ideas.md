# Ideas and polls

This is where Gregtorio Continued's direction gets decided with you. The maintainer posts polls here, and anyone can bring their own ideas:

- Reply under a poll or start a thread with your own proposal (what, why, and how it would play).
- Polls have fixed answers; if none fits, pick "Something else" and describe yours in a thread.
- Results shape the plan, but they are not binding votes: balance and feasibility get the last word.

Bugs belong in {{#gregtorio-help}} or on GitHub, not here.
