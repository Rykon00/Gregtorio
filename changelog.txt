Version: 0.4.0
  Features:
    - Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge (UMV, 11x11, 1.31 GW): makes neutronium, cosmic neutronium, infinity, transcendent metal, spacetime and universium from the inputs of their fusion recipes with two thirds of the input per ingot and in a third of the reactor time, burning an excited dimensionally transcendent catalyst. The fusion reactors keep their recipes. Technology Dimensionally Transcendent Plasma Forge (UMV science).
    - Excited dimensionally transcendent catalysts, made in the forge: crude (helium, iron, calcium and niobium plasma) and resplendent (UXV technology Resplendent Plasma Forge Catalyst: the crude catalyst with boron, sulfur, nitrogen, zinc and titanium plasma), with which the forge makes twice the batch in the same time for less catalyst per ingot.
    - Quantum Force Transformer (UMV, 9x9, 1.31 GW): the platinum line (platinum and palladium metallic powder, iridium metal residue, rarest metal mixture, crude rhodium residue, iridium group sludge) and the naquadah line (naquadah and enriched naquadah oxide mixture) in one step, one recipe per main output, with nitrogen plasma. Technology Quantum Force Transformer (UMV science).
    - Godforge (UXV, 13x13, 2.62 GW), after the stargate with MAX science: raw star matter from hydrogen and helium (the fuel every other godforge recipe burns), lead, thorium and naquadria plasma, universium from half the spacetime and flerovium (technology Godforge: Molten Module), and tachyon rich temporal and spatially enlarged fluid from spacetime and magmatter from the melt of neutronium, tritanium, cosmic neutronium, draconium, infinity, rhugnor or flerovium (technology Godforge: Exotic Module).
    - Stellar Plasma Forge Catalyst (MAX science): the stellar catalyst, with which the plasma forge makes four times the crude batch in the same time.
    - The MAX tier (MAX science): magmatter parts and cable, the Planck line and the MAX circuit, the MAX components, casing and hull, the maximum voltage coil, MAX energy and dynamo hatch, the 23 MAX machines and 13 MAX multiblock upgrades, and the MAX Large Plasma Turbine (20.97 GW). A second MAX science pack recipe makes 100 packs from MAX parts and magmatter; the stargate recipe stays.
    - Godforge Upgrades: an infinite technology (MAX science) that gives the godforge's star matter, universium and magmatter recipes 5 % productivity per level, next to the further levels of Victory the long-term goal after winning.
    - ME network rework, part 1 (issue #68): the ME network now works like Applied Energistics 2 instead of being a logistic network. ME cables (placed with the fluix cable item) connect the ME blocks on all four sides, ME blocks that touch each other connect without a cable, and a network runs with exactly one powered ME Controller (two in one network are a conflict and stop it). The controller uses 120 kW plus 4 kW for every drive, interface, bus, provider and fluid block.
    - ME Underground Cable (technology ME Network): carries the ME network under up to 10 tiles to another underground cable facing it, like an underground pipe; above ground an end connects only on the side opposite its arrow; a dashed line on the ground shows a connected pair. ME cables and underground cables can be walked over.
    - ME Drive: placed empty, takes up to 10 storage cells (open it, click a slot with a cell in hand; clicking the drive with a cell in hand uses the first free slot). A light per cell shows its fill state. Mined drives keep their cells, destroyed drives drop them.
    - Storage cells keep their items: a cell taken out of a drive carries its contents (listed in its tooltip) into any other drive. Capacity as in AE2: 1k to 256k bytes, 63 item types per cell, 8 items per byte plus some bytes per type. Every quality is stored; items that spoil, items with an inventory or grid, blueprints and damaged items are not.
    - ME Terminal: status line (bytes and types used, drives, cells, controller power), search and sort, left click takes a stack into the hand (with something in hand: stores it), right click one item, shift click a stack into the inventory, and a row with your inventory to store items from it.
    - ME Interface: up to 9 config rows (item and amount, set in its window): the network keeps that amount of each item in the interface (fills up, takes back the surplus), everything else put in is imported. Works with inserters and belts like a chest; "Open inventory" shows the container. The config is kept in blueprints and copied with the entity settings.
    - ME Import Bus and ME Export Bus (technology ME Network): rotatable blocks that move items between the network and the machine or chest they face, up to 5 item filters each.
    - ME network rework, part 2 (issue #68): fluid storage cells go into the ME Drive like item cells (a drive can hold both) and keep their fluid when taken out. Capacity like the item cells: 1k to 256k bytes, up to 18 fluids per cell, 8 units per byte (a 1k fluid cell holds 8128 units of one fluid).
    - The ME Terminal shows the fluids in its grid after the items (with search and sort) and the bytes and types of the fluid cells in its status line.
    - ME Fluid Import Bus and ME Fluid Export Bus (technology ME Fluid Storage): rotatable blocks that empty a machine's output or a tank into the network, or fill a machine or tank with their filtered fluids, 1000 units about every quarter second.
    - ME network rework, part 3 (issue #68): every ME block has its own window in one style (title bar, close and drag), opened by clicking the block: ME Drive, storage cell, ME Controller (network status), Pattern Provider (its machines and the furnace recipe), Crafting CPU (its jobs with progress and cancel), Level Maintainer (with the circuit condition), Circuit Interface (filters, output on/off), Fluid Interface, ME Interface and the buses. They replace the panels next to the lamp, combinator and tank windows and the game's window of those blocks. Amounts show as 1.2k, 12k, 1.5M, 2.5G everywhere.
    - The ME Terminal is the hub of the network: Storage (with a filter for items or fluids), Crafting (pick an item or fluid, amount, a plan preview that shows what is missing and what is taken from storage, Craft), Jobs (progress, cancel) and Cells (the drives by priority with their cells and fill; click a drive or a cell to open it, Back returns to the terminal).
    - Cell partitions (AE2's partitioned cells): a storage cell can be restricted to chosen items (with quality) or fluids in its window ("From contents" takes what it holds). A partitioned cell takes only those and gets them before other cells of the same priority; the partition stays with the cell.
    - Drive priorities (-1000 to 1000) in the drive window: items and fluids go into the drives of the highest priority first and are taken from the lowest priority first (and from cells that are not partitioned first). Priority and partitions are kept in blueprints, settings paste and clones of a drive.
  Changes:
    - The ME network no longer uses Factorio's logistic network: the ME Controller is no roboport (it no longer extends logistic or construction coverage and no longer reads the network on its circuit connection; use the ME Circuit Interface), the ME Drive is no storage chest and the ME Interface no requester chest. Robots no longer store items in ME Drives.
    - ME Drives are no longer crafted with four cells: the drive chassis is the ME Drive, the cells go into it. Old drive items cannot be crafted any more; placing one builds an ME Drive with its four cells. Storage cells stack to 1.
    - ME Crafting CPU and Co-Processing Crafting CPU take two storage components instead of two storage cells (a cell may carry items).
    - ME Interfaces of older saves: each filtered slot becomes a config row of one stack (the same item in several slots adds up) when the interface first works after the update, and the slot filters are cleared; blueprints with the old filters are converted when built. Open ME windows and panels of older versions are closed on the update.
    - ME Fluid Drives are replaced by fluid storage cells in ME Drives: when a save is loaded, every ME Fluid Drive becomes an ME Drive with four fluid cells of its tier holding its fluid, recovered fluid of destroyed drives goes into the fluid cells of its network, and a fluid drive item that carries fluid keeps its item while its fluid comes as fluid cells next to it. Nothing is lost; old fluid drive items can no longer be crafted or taken apart, placing one gives an ME Drive with its fluid cells. Recovered fluid and the "Take over" button are gone: a fluid cell keeps its fluid, a destroyed drive drops its cells.
    - Saves: old ME networks are converted when the save is loaded: every old drive becomes an ME Drive with four cells of its tier holding its items, the controller and interfaces are replaced in place, ME cables are laid from the controller to every block of the network, and further controllers of the same network go into it as items. Items the cells cannot hold go into chests next to the controller; blocks no cable can reach are named in the chat. Fluid drives, CPUs, patterns and running jobs are kept.
  Graphics:
    - ME cable (a picture for every combination of connected sides), ME Drive with ten cell bays, ME Controller, ME Import and Export Bus, drawn with Pillow from the existing GregTech based ME sprites.
    - ME Fluid Import and Export Bus: the item buses in fluid blue with a pipe ring.
    - The plasma forge and the quantum force transformer, their parts and technologies use GregTech textures (dimensionally transcendent casing, dimensional bridge, plasma forge screen; QFT coil and controller).
    - The godforge, the MAX tier's machines, hatches, components, magmatter parts, the Planck circuits and the new technologies use GregTech textures (godforge casings and controller, MAX hull, GT's MAX components and coil, magmatter icon set, the Planck-scale circuit).
  Bugfixes:
    - The main menu no longer stops with "The scenario level caused a non-recoverable error" (LuaEntity was invalid). Menu simulations that research every technology (research_all_technologies) did not get the bonuses of the vanilla weapon and health technologies that Gregtorio disables, so the character of the "biter base laser defense" simulation died and its script failed. Those simulations now get the bonuses of the disabled technologies and run as in vanilla; no simulation is removed.

Version: 0.3.2
Date: 2026-09-30
  Features:
    - Lapotronic Energy Orbs technology (ZPM): the lapotronic energy orb and a second recipe for the lapotronic energy orb cluster in the circuit assembler (eight orbs, qubit CPU chips, naquadah alloy).
    - High density plutonium (wrapped plutonium ingot, high density plutonium nugget from the implosion compressor, high density plutonium), unlocked by Naquadah Fuels.
    - Super coolant (LuV technology Super Coolant): ledox and callisto ice from a new end microminer recipe, mixed with lapis coolant.
    - Fluxed electrum (ZPM), bedrockium (UV) and quantium (UHV), each with its technology; bedrockium and quantium come from new end microminer recipes.
    - Space coolant cells (UHV): the 180k, 540k and 1080k space cells and the 1080k super coolant cell.
    - Large Plasma Turbines for UHV (655.36 MW), UEV (1.31 GW), UIV (2.62 GW), UMV (5.24 GW) and UXV (10.49 GW), each an upgrade of the turbine one tier below with the dynamo hatch and a hull of its tier, unlocked by the technologies UHV to UXV Plasma Turbine. One fusion reactor feeds about 12 turbines of its own tier: MK3 UV, MK4 UEV, MK5 UIV turbines.
    - ME Level Maintainer (technology ME Automation, EV): keeps an amount of an item or fluid in the ME network by starting a crafting job for the difference, one job at a time. Its lamp circuit condition switches it on and off; it can take the amount from a circuit signal.
    - ME Circuit Interface (technology ME Automation): puts the items and fluids of the ME network, or only the filtered ones, onto the circuit network.
    - ME Co-Processing Crafting CPU (IV, two jobs at once, twice as fast) and ME Quantum Crafting CPU (LuV, four jobs at once, four times as fast). The upgrade planner swaps Crafting CPU tiers.
  Changes:
    - The UHV to UXV dynamo hatches are also unlocked by the plasma turbine technology of their tier.
    - Plutonium based liquid fuel takes one high density plutonium (like GregTech) instead of 64 plutonium 239 dust.
    - GregTech materials instead of stand-ins: grade 5 water takes 100 super coolant (grade 5 plants stop until you make it), grade 7 water super coolant instead of cryogenic helium; the UEV to UXV energy and dynamo hatches take 2, 4, 6 and 8 1080k super coolant cells, the UIV to UXV ones super coolant instead of cryogenic helium.
    - The UV voltage coil takes fine fluxed electrum wire, the UHV emitter and sensor fluxed electrum foil, the fusion reactor MK3 controller molten fluxed electrum, naquadah based fuel MK2 32 fluxed electrum dust (was 16 naquadria dust).
    - The UHV components take bedrockium cable instead of tritanium cable, the UEV machine casing bedrockium plates; the UEV components take molten quantium instead of molten cosmic neutronium, the UMV components quantium cable instead of spacetime cable.
    - Removed draft content that could never be made and that belongs to other mods (Thaumcraft infused gold, thaumium, magic essence, void and shadow metal, ichorium; force, astral titanium and runite plasma; the atomic separation catalyst, orundum and the naquadah fuel cracking).
    - ME Fluid Drives: recovered fluid flows into the fluid drives of its network by itself as soon as they have room (for example after an export), not only into a newly placed drive. The chat reports it once when all of it is back.
    - ME Fluid Interface: mode, fluid and fill level are copied with the entity settings (shift right click, shift left click) and kept in blueprints and clones.
    - The Crafting tab of the ME Terminal shows the job slots of the network's Crafting CPUs.
    - ME Fluid Drives: upgrading a loaded drive (upgrade planner with robots, or fast replace by hand) moves its fluid into the new drive; what does not fit goes into the network, the rest becomes recovered fluid. The old drive item comes back empty.
  Balancing:
    - Fusion power: one Fusion Reactor MK1 makes about 1 GW of helium plasma (12.5 per second, 12 LuV plasma turbines) instead of 5.12 GW; an MK2 makes about 2 GW, an MK3 about 4 GW. The cheap plasmas give 11x to 17x the energy of their whole input chain instead of up to 70x.
    - Helium-3: 50 per compressed end stone in 200 s instead of 100 in 40 s.
    - Fusion recipes take longer: helium plasma 10 s in the MK1 (was 2 s from helium-3, 4 s from tritium), nitrogen plasma 8 s (was 4 s), niobium plasma 20 s (was 8 s), tin plasma 24 s (was 8 s) in the MK2.
    - Sulfur plasma takes 72 molten lithium and 72 molten aluminium (was 16 each) and 16 s in the MK2 (was 8 s); iron plasma takes 72 molten silicon and 72 molten magnesium (was 16 each) and 8 s in the MK3 (was 2 s).
    - Research from UV to victory costs far fewer units: UV technologies about a quarter (for example UV Energy Hatches 450 instead of 2000), UHV and UEV about a fourteenth, UIV about a 25th, UMV about a 32nd, UXV and the Stargate technology about a 60th (Stargate 50 instead of 3000). Each technology now takes about as long as a ZPM technology in a factory of its tier.
    - Victory takes 15 units per level instead of 1000 (15 * 2^(L-1)): level 1 costs one stargate and a few packs of each tier; the 1000 MAX packs of one stargate last for the first six levels.
    - UV motor: 64 fine americium wires instead of 384 and 8 long neutronium rods instead of 4.
    - Large neutronium gear: 57.6 molten neutronium (4 ingots) instead of 576, like the large tritanium gear; the UV piston, robot arm and everything with them get cheaper.
    - Stargate parts take fewer parts: the ring block has no UXV field generator, the base and power unit one UXV field generator each (was 4 and 2), the controller 8 UXV circuits (was 32), the chevron block its emitter and pistons only through the chevron upgrade; less molten universium and fewer frame parts and radiation containment plates. The stargate needs 2 UXV field generators and about 200 UXV circuits instead of 14 and 620.
  Graphics:
    - Real icons for 393 items that had recolored placeholder icons, from GregTech textures: LuV to UXV components, hatches, casings and hulls, the wetware, bio, optical, exotic and temporal circuits, stem cells, wafers, power ICs, SMDs, fusion and coil blocks, stargate parts, coolant cells, and the new metals in GregTech's material icons and colours. Their technologies show the new icons.
    - Fusion Reactor MK4 and MK5 have their own look (the fusion casings and coils of GregTech's compact fusion reactors MK-IV and MK-V) instead of the MK3 one.
    - UHV to UXV machines show the machine casing of their tier around the machine; Large Plasma Turbines and Large Naquadah Reactors show the dynamo hatches of their tier instead of a colour tint.
    - IV to UXV upgrade multiblocks (electric blast furnace, vacuum freezer, large chemical reactor, microverse projector, distillation towers, implosion compressor, cracker, multismelter, pyrolyse oven, greenhouse, drilling rig, alloy blast smelter) show two energy hatches of their tier on the EV art, and their item icons carry the hatch as a badge, so the tiers can be told apart.
    - Graphics from GT5-Unofficial (LGPL-3.0) and NewHorizonsCoreMod (GPL-3.0), see the License section of the README.

Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-09-30
  Features:
    - Endgame power: plasmas have fuel values; Large Plasma Turbines (LuV, ZPM, UV) burn them, and a Turbine Output Hatch next to a turbine collects the cooled fluid (helium, nitrogen, ...).
    - Naquadah fuel line (naquadah based fuel MK1 to MK3, excited uranium and plutonium fuels) and Large Naquadah Reactors from UV to UXV.
    - Dynamo hatches from LuV to UXV.
    - ME Fluid Drives: the fluid of a destroyed drive goes into the other drives of its network, the rest is kept as "recovered fluid" that the next drive placed in that network takes over (also with the "Take over" button in the drive window). Every step is reported in chat.
    - ME Pattern Provider: open it to choose the recipe for the furnaces next to it. A furnace is an autocrafting pattern right away and no longer has to smelt the recipe once by hand. The choice is copied with the provider's settings and kept in blueprints.
    - Water purification grades 7 (degassed, UHV) and 8 (quark extraction with a reusable Quark Creation Catalyst, UEV) in the Water Purification Plant.
    - Femto and Atto Power ICs (FPIC, APIC) and complex SMDs (transistor, resistor, capacitor, diode, inductor) with the technologies Femto Power ICs, Atto Power ICs and Complex SMDs.
  Changes:
    - The UEV energy and dynamo hatches and the fusion reactor MK4 controller use FPICs; the UIV to UXV energy and dynamo hatches and the MK5 controller use APICs instead of Quantum Power ICs. Their technologies need the new chip technology.
    - The wetware mainframe, the bio supercomputer and mainframe and the assembly, supercomputer and mainframe of the optical, exotic and temporal lines use complex SMDs instead of advanced SMDs (a quarter of the count). The wetware mainframes technology needs Complex SMDs.
    - Taking the cells out of a loaded ME Fluid Drive is hand crafting only; its fluid goes into the network you stand in. Assemblers set to this recipe lose it.
    - The Crafting tab of the ME Terminal counts furnaces next to a pattern provider that have no recipe.
    - The crafting menu lists the recipes that only a machine can make once they are unlocked (red background, like in vanilla), for example the zinc recipes and the 4x Resistor. Fluid voiding and the replaced vanilla recipes stay hidden. Startup setting "Show machine recipes in the crafting menu" (default on) to hide them again.
    - The start hints mention Factoriopedia (Alt+click): "Alternative recipes" and "Made in".
    - The 23 quality-of-life technologies that could not be researched are now researchable with Gregtorio science packs: Bulk Inserter (MV), Stack Inserter (HV), Inserter capacity bonus 1-7 (MV to IV), Express transport belt (HV), Turbo transport belt (EV), Transport belt capacity 1-2 (EV, IV), Worker robot speed 1-7 (MV to ZPM) and Worker robot cargo size 1-3 (MV to EV). Bulk and stack inserters and the express and turbo belts, undergrounds and splitters get GregTech recipes (robot arms, conveyor modules, pistons, circuits) instead of lubricant, carbon fiber, jelly and tungsten.
  Bugfixes:
    - Large Plasma Turbines only burn plasmas and Large Naquadah Reactors only naquadah and excited fuels. A wrong fluid (for example steam) stops the generator with "Wrong fuel" instead of being burnt; an empty generator shows "No fuel".
    - Turbine Output Hatch: a Large Plasma Turbine now returns exactly one unit of cooled fluid per unit of plasma it burns, at any load (before, up to 12% went missing at partial or changing load). Cooled fluid that does not fit into full hatches waits in the turbine instead of being lost.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-09-29
  Info:
    - Published on the mod portal as "gregtorio-continued" (Gregtorio Continued). The name "Gregtorio" belongs to the original author, Damien Reave, who can no longer update it. This continuation keeps his work and credits him.
  Features:
    - The GregTech: New Horizons progression is complete up to victory: LuV endgame (naquadah line, bacterial vat, crystal processors, fusion reactor MK1), then ZPM, UV, UHV, UEV, UIV, UMV and UXV with their circuits, components, energy hatches, machines, multiblock upgrades and science packs.
    - Fusion reactors MK1 to MK5 with their plasmas and endgame metals (europium, americium, tritanium, cosmic neutronium, infinity, transcendent metal, spacetime, universium).
    - Wetware, bio and optical circuit lines; water purification plant with grades 1 to 6 and the NPIC, PPIC and QPIC chips.
    - Stargate and MAX science pack; researching Victory level 1 wins the game (you can continue playing).
    - ME network (AE2): ME Controller, ME Drives with storage cells (1k to 256k), ME Interface and ME Terminal on top of the logistic network.
    - ME autocrafting: ME Pattern Provider (the recipe of the machine next to it becomes a pattern, GT machines included), ME Molecular Assembler, ME Crafting CPU and a Crafting tab in the terminal with plan preview, missing items and job list.
    - ME fluids: ME Fluid Drives with fluid storage cells, ME Fluid Interface (import and export), fluids in the terminal and fluid recipes in autocrafting.
  Changes:
    - Molds are no longer used up: alloy smelters, fluid solidifiers and extruders have a mold slot, and their recipes need a mold there. Existing machines get their mold once.
    - The Manual Inserter runs on manual labor instead of for free.
    - The LV science pack is made from an LV motor, LV piston, primitive circuit and tin cables (2 packs) instead of a belt and an inserter.
    - Buildings that burn manual labor show the fist in the empty fuel slot and "No manual labor" instead of "No fuel".
    - Placeholder recipes that turned stone into endgame science packs are replaced by real recipes.
  Graphics:
    - Machine sprites for the new tiers and technology icons instead of the "NYI" placeholder, generated from GregTech 5 Unofficial textures (LGPL-3.0); fluid technologies show a GT fluid cell. Many item icons are still recolored placeholders.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-09-28
  Features:
    - IV is playable: the IV science pack is unlocked, 23 IV basic machines, 13 IV multiblock upgrades and all 17 GT++ industrial multiblocks are buildable.
    - LuV is playable: HSS-S parts, rhodium-plated palladium, the LuV Assembly Line, LuV components, 37 LuV machines, a LuV science pack recipe and 9 LuV technologies.
    - EV multiblocks that could be crafted but not placed now work: Alloy Blast Smelter, Extreme Entity Crusher, Ender IO machines (Slice'n'Splice, Soul Binder, Powered Spawner) and the HV Large Sifter.
    - New sprites and icons built from GregTech 5 Unofficial textures (LGPL-3.0).
    - English names for 520 items, buildings, fluids and technologies that showed "Unknown key".
  Changes:
    - Vanilla resource patches (iron, copper, stone, coal, uranium, oil, Space Age resources) no longer spawn and can no longer be mined in existing saves; Gregtorio gets all raw materials from its own mines. The admin command /gregtorio-remove-vanilla-ores removes the leftover patches.
    - Machines accept all recipe categories of their own and lower tiers (e.g. the EV Fluid Solidifier can now make MV/HV/EV recipes).
    - Chemical baths have one fluid input and one fluid output (the platinum line needs an output).
    - Iridium dust and HPIC wafers are made in the EV chemical reactor (every IV machine needs them).
    - Intermediate recipes that no technology unlocked are now unlocked together with the recipes that need them.
  Bugfixes:
    - Added missing recipes: zirconium dust (Alloy Blast Smelter) and cryogenic helium.
    - Gregtorio's own tungsten carbide technology was disabled together with the Space Age one of the same name.
    - Industrial Wire Factory needed itself as an ingredient; Industrial Wire Factory and TurboCan Pro had no unlocking technology.
    - Existing saves: newly added unlocks of already researched technologies are applied on load.
