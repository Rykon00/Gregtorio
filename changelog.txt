Version: 0.3.2
  Features:
    - Lapotronic Energy Orbs technology (ZPM): the lapotronic energy orb and a second recipe for the lapotronic energy orb cluster in the circuit assembler (eight orbs, qubit CPU chips, naquadah alloy).
    - High density plutonium (wrapped plutonium ingot, high density plutonium nugget from the implosion compressor, high density plutonium), unlocked by Naquadah Fuels.
    - Large Plasma Turbines for UHV (655.36 MW), UEV (1.31 GW), UIV (2.62 GW), UMV (5.24 GW) and UXV (10.49 GW), each an upgrade of the turbine one tier below with the dynamo hatch and a hull of its tier, unlocked by the technologies UHV to UXV Plasma Turbine. One fusion reactor feeds about 12 turbines of its own tier: MK3 UV, MK4 UEV, MK5 UIV turbines.
  Changes:
    - The UHV to UXV dynamo hatches are also unlocked by the plasma turbine technology of their tier.
    - Plutonium based liquid fuel takes one high density plutonium (like GregTech) instead of 64 plutonium 239 dust.
    - Removed draft content that could never be made and that belongs to other mods (Thaumcraft infused gold, thaumium, magic essence, void and shadow metal, ichorium; force, astral titanium and runite plasma; the atomic separation catalyst, orundum and the naquadah fuel cracking).
  Balancing:
    - Fusion power: one Fusion Reactor MK1 makes about 1 GW of helium plasma (12.5 per second, 12 LuV plasma turbines) instead of 5.12 GW; an MK2 makes about 2 GW, an MK3 about 4 GW. The cheap plasmas give 11x to 17x the energy of their whole input chain instead of up to 70x.
    - Helium-3: 50 per compressed end stone in 200 s instead of 100 in 40 s.
    - Fusion recipes take longer: helium plasma 10 s in the MK1 (was 2 s from helium-3, 4 s from tritium), nitrogen plasma 8 s (was 4 s), niobium plasma 20 s (was 8 s), tin plasma 24 s (was 8 s) in the MK2.
    - Sulfur plasma takes 72 molten lithium and 72 molten aluminium (was 16 each) and 16 s in the MK2 (was 8 s); iron plasma takes 72 molten silicon and 72 molten magnesium (was 16 each) and 8 s in the MK3 (was 2 s).

Version: 0.3.1
Date: 2026-09-30
  Features:
    - Endgame power: plasmas have fuel values; Large Plasma Turbines (LuV, ZPM, UV) burn them, and a Turbine Output Hatch next to a turbine collects the cooled fluid (helium, nitrogen, ...).
    - Naquadah fuel line (naquadah based fuel MK1 to MK3, excited uranium and plutonium fuels) and Large Naquadah Reactors from UV to UXV.
    - Dynamo hatches from LuV to UXV.
    - ME Fluid Drives: the fluid of a destroyed drive goes into the other drives of its network, the rest is kept as "recovered fluid" that the next drive placed in that network takes over (also with the "Take over" button in the drive window). Every step is reported in chat.
    - ME Pattern Provider: open it to choose the recipe for the furnaces next to it. A furnace is an autocrafting pattern right away and no longer has to smelt the recipe once by hand. The choice is copied with the provider's settings and kept in blueprints.
    - Water purification grades 7 (degassed, UHV) and 8 (quark extraction with a reusable Quark Creation Catalyst, UEV) in the Water Purification Plant.
    - Femto and Atto Power ICs (FPIC, APIC) and complex SMDs (transistor, resistor, capacitor, diode, inductor) with the technologies Femto Power ICs, Atto Power ICs and Complex SMDs.
  Changes:
    - The UEV energy and dynamo hatches and the fusion reactor MK4 controller use FPICs; the UIV to UXV energy and dynamo hatches and the MK5 controller use APICs instead of Quantum Power ICs. Their technologies need the new chip technology.
    - The wetware mainframe, the bio supercomputer and mainframe and the assembly, supercomputer and mainframe of the optical, exotic and temporal lines use complex SMDs instead of advanced SMDs (a quarter of the count). The wetware mainframes technology needs Complex SMDs.
    - Taking the cells out of a loaded ME Fluid Drive is hand crafting only; its fluid goes into the network you stand in. Assemblers set to this recipe lose it.
    - The Crafting tab of the ME Terminal counts furnaces next to a pattern provider that have no recipe.
    - The crafting menu lists the recipes that only a machine can make once they are unlocked (red background, like in vanilla), for example the zinc recipes and the 4x Resistor. Fluid voiding and the replaced vanilla recipes stay hidden. Startup setting "Show machine recipes in the crafting menu" (default on) to hide them again.
    - The start hints mention Factoriopedia (Alt+click): "Alternative recipes" and "Made in".
    - The 23 quality-of-life technologies that could not be researched are now researchable with Gregtorio science packs: Bulk Inserter (MV), Stack Inserter (HV), Inserter capacity bonus 1-7 (MV to IV), Express transport belt (HV), Turbo transport belt (EV), Transport belt capacity 1-2 (EV, IV), Worker robot speed 1-7 (MV to ZPM) and Worker robot cargo size 1-3 (MV to EV). Bulk and stack inserters and the express and turbo belts, undergrounds and splitters get GregTech recipes (robot arms, conveyor modules, pistons, circuits) instead of lubricant, carbon fiber, jelly and tungsten.
  Bugfixes:
    - Large Plasma Turbines only burn plasmas and Large Naquadah Reactors only naquadah and excited fuels. A wrong fluid (for example steam) stops the generator with "Wrong fuel" instead of being burnt; an empty generator shows "No fuel".
    - Turbine Output Hatch: a Large Plasma Turbine now returns exactly one unit of cooled fluid per unit of plasma it burns, at any load (before, up to 12% went missing at partial or changing load). Cooled fluid that does not fit into full hatches waits in the turbine instead of being lost.

Version: 0.3.0
Date: 2026-09-29
  Info:
    - Published on the mod portal as "gregtorio-continued" (Gregtorio Continued). The name "Gregtorio" belongs to the original author, Damien Reave, who can no longer update it. This continuation keeps his work and credits him.
  Features:
    - The GregTech: New Horizons progression is complete up to victory: LuV endgame (naquadah line, bacterial vat, crystal processors, fusion reactor MK1), then ZPM, UV, UHV, UEV, UIV, UMV and UXV with their circuits, components, energy hatches, machines, multiblock upgrades and science packs.
    - Fusion reactors MK1 to MK5 with their plasmas and endgame metals (europium, americium, tritanium, cosmic neutronium, infinity, transcendent metal, spacetime, universium).
    - Wetware, bio and optical circuit lines; water purification plant with grades 1 to 6 and the NPIC, PPIC and QPIC chips.
    - Stargate and MAX science pack; researching Victory level 1 wins the game (you can continue playing).
    - ME network (AE2): ME Controller, ME Drives with storage cells (1k to 256k), ME Interface and ME Terminal on top of the logistic network.
    - ME autocrafting: ME Pattern Provider (the recipe of the machine next to it becomes a pattern, GT machines included), ME Molecular Assembler, ME Crafting CPU and a Crafting tab in the terminal with plan preview, missing items and job list.
    - ME fluids: ME Fluid Drives with fluid storage cells, ME Fluid Interface (import and export), fluids in the terminal and fluid recipes in autocrafting.
  Changes:
    - Molds are no longer used up: alloy smelters, fluid solidifiers and extruders have a mold slot, and their recipes need a mold there. Existing machines get their mold once.
    - The Manual Inserter runs on manual labor instead of for free.
    - The LV science pack is made from an LV motor, LV piston, primitive circuit and tin cables (2 packs) instead of a belt and an inserter.
    - Buildings that burn manual labor show the fist in the empty fuel slot and "No manual labor" instead of "No fuel".
    - Placeholder recipes that turned stone into endgame science packs are replaced by real recipes.
  Graphics:
    - Machine sprites for the new tiers and technology icons instead of the "NYI" placeholder, generated from GregTech 5 Unofficial textures (LGPL-3.0); fluid technologies show a GT fluid cell. Many item icons are still recolored placeholders.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-09-28
  Features:
    - IV is playable: the IV science pack is unlocked, 23 IV basic machines, 13 IV multiblock upgrades and all 17 GT++ industrial multiblocks are buildable.
    - LuV is playable: HSS-S parts, rhodium-plated palladium, the LuV Assembly Line, LuV components, 37 LuV machines, a LuV science pack recipe and 9 LuV technologies.
    - EV multiblocks that could be crafted but not placed now work: Alloy Blast Smelter, Extreme Entity Crusher, Ender IO machines (Slice'n'Splice, Soul Binder, Powered Spawner) and the HV Large Sifter.
    - New sprites and icons built from GregTech 5 Unofficial textures (LGPL-3.0).
    - English names for 520 items, buildings, fluids and technologies that showed "Unknown key".
  Changes:
    - Vanilla resource patches (iron, copper, stone, coal, uranium, oil, Space Age resources) no longer spawn and can no longer be mined in existing saves; Gregtorio gets all raw materials from its own mines. The admin command /gregtorio-remove-vanilla-ores removes the leftover patches.
    - Machines accept all recipe categories of their own and lower tiers (e.g. the EV Fluid Solidifier can now make MV/HV/EV recipes).
    - Chemical baths have one fluid input and one fluid output (the platinum line needs an output).
    - Iridium dust and HPIC wafers are made in the EV chemical reactor (every IV machine needs them).
    - Intermediate recipes that no technology unlocked are now unlocked together with the recipes that need them.
  Bugfixes:
    - Added missing recipes: zirconium dust (Alloy Blast Smelter) and cryogenic helium.
    - Gregtorio's own tungsten carbide technology was disabled together with the Space Age one of the same name.
    - Industrial Wire Factory needed itself as an ingredient; Industrial Wire Factory and TurboCan Pro had no unlocking technology.
    - Existing saves: newly added unlocks of already researched technologies are applied on load.
