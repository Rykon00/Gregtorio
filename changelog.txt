Version: 0.5.9
  Changes:
    - The torch gives light without power (issue #555): it is a building of its own now, as in GT New Horizons, lit from dusk to dawn with a small warm light from the steam age on, 1 x 1 and walked past. The recipe Torch (a stick and coal) and the new Torch from Charcoal give 4 torches; the technology Torches stays where it was. The torch made vanilla's electric lamp before, which stays as the Lamp (it needs power, a circuit network sets its colour) with an LV assembler recipe of its own (an LV circuit, glass, a steel plate and 2 tin cables) with Automation 2. Placed lamps and lamp items of existing saves become torches (they all came from the torch recipe).
    - The colour drop-downs of the Ender Chest and the Ender Tank list the 16 colours alphabetically (issue #545). Only the order shown changed: chests and tanks of existing saves and blueprints keep their colour codes and still share with the ones built now.
    - Every ME item is in ME Network's own inventory tab "ME Network" (issue #519, ME Network issue #474): the storage housings and the storage components 1k to 16384k moved there from the circuit assembler and assembler rows of the machine recipes tab; their recipes stay in those machine rows. The ME recipes that were in the tab already (buses, monitors, terminals, pattern blocks, cards, void cells and others) sit in the row of their item now, so the crafting window shows each beside its item. The Group Filter Card matches subgroup and group names: a filter text that named an old row may match other items now.
  Bugfixes:
    - The Dimension Mine comes with Primitive Electronic Circuit Assemblies, the technology of its first recipe (the Copper Tin Vein), instead of Craft Wooden Tools (issue #556): it was a building without a recipe until then. Placed Dimension Mines stay; a save that researched Craft Wooden Tools but not Primitive Electronic Circuit Assemblies cannot craft new ones until it does. Every dimension mine now comes with its first recipe or its own technology, whichever is later, also when vein recipes move. The same for other machines that had no recipe yet: the LV Fluid Extractor comes with Concrete (before: Extractor, which Concrete needs now), the LV Laser Engraver with MV Machines (before: Circuit Assembler; the ruby, diamond and red garnet engravings come with it, before with Laser Engraver), the HV Cracker and its controller with Oil Processing (before: Bauxite Slurry Process, which is now a prerequisite of Reach the Moon instead of Oil Processing), and the EV to ZPM Chemical Dehydrator technologies need Hydrazine Rocket Fuels, which has the first recipes they run.
    - Three technologies get the prerequisites their recipes need (issue #517): IV Components needs Iridium and RTM Alloy Coils and unlocks the IV Electric Blast Furnace (before: IV Machines), so the hot iridium ingot of the IV sensor's and emitter's iridium rods can be made there; Precise Electronic Unit Mk-III needs Bio Processors (the Hi-Computation Stations Mk-IV and Mk-V take Qubit CPU chips and bio processing units); Dimensionally Shifted Superfluid needs Radox Polymer (super heavy radox). The electric blast furnace's titanium recipes with neon, krypton and xenon come with EV Machines instead of End Steel, which had no titanium before it.
    - Ender Chests and Ender Tanks built from a blueprint keep their colour code (issue #546): a blueprint (also one made with copy or cut) stores the code of both, and the chest or tank built from it (by hand, by robots or when the blueprint is pasted over one) gets it. Before, a chest built from a blueprint a player placed had white, white, white and a tank never kept its code. Blueprints made before this version still give the Ender Chest its code when a script builds them; make them again to keep the codes in every case and for the tanks.
    - The Stone Pickaxe has a use again (issue #547): since the vein recipes of the mines were removed in 0.5.6, nothing took it after the 10 that research Craft Stone Tools. Craft Stone Tools unlocks a Dig Site recipe "Mining Cobblestone With Stone Pickaxe": 1 Stone Pickaxe and 4 manual labor give 64 stone in 10 s, between the Wooden Pickaxe's recipe (8 manual labor) and the Iron Pickaxe's (2 manual labor).

    - New sprites and icons built from GregTech 5 Unofficial textures (LGPL-3.0).
    - English names for 520 items, buildings, fluids and technologies that showed "Unknown key".
  Changes:
    - Vanilla resource patches (iron, copper, stone, coal, uranium, oil, Space Age resources) no longer spawn and can no longer be mined in existing saves; Gregtorio gets all raw materials from its own mines. The admin command /gregtorio-remove-vanilla-ores removes the leftover patches.
    - Machines accept all recipe categories of their own and lower tiers (e.g. the EV Fluid Solidifier can now make MV/HV/EV recipes).
    - Chemical baths have one fluid input and one fluid output (the platinum line needs an output).
    - Iridium dust and HPIC wafers are made in the EV chemical reactor (every IV machine needs them).
    - Intermediate recipes that no technology unlocked are now unlocked together with the recipes that need them.
  Bugfixes:
    - Added missing recipes: zirconium dust (Alloy Blast Smelter) and cryogenic helium.
    - Gregtorio's own tungsten carbide technology was disabled together with the Space Age one of the same name.
    - Industrial Wire Factory needed itself as an ingredient; Industrial Wire Factory and TurboCan Pro had no unlocking technology.
    - Existing saves: newly added unlocks of already researched technologies are applied on load.
